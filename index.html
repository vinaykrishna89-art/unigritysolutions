Our tool

Beta Chrome extension

TagVault — in-session analytics QA, with evidence you can hand over.

TagVault is a Chrome extension for analytics QA. As you click through a site, it captures Adobe Analytics, GA4, and marketing-pixel requests, checks them, and produces a sign-off artifact. Everything stays on your machine. There is no telemetry.

Who it is for

Analytics implementation leads, solution architects, and QA analysts at agencies and in-house teams — especially for release QA, migrations (including Adobe Analytics to Customer Journey Analytics and Web SDK work), and consent or PII audits.

The problem it addresses

  • Manual tag QA is slow and often leaves no proof
  • Regressions slip between releases
  • Duplicate or missing hits go unnoticed
  • PII can appear in tracking requests
  • Teams lack a clean sign-off deliverable

It complements scheduled governance. TagVault is built for the in-session sign-off moment on a real journey. It sits alongside platforms such as ObservePoint and DataTrue — it is not a replacement for estate-wide crawling or monitoring.

Key features

Multi-vendor capture

Adobe Analytics, Web SDK, Launch, GA4 (including first-party and server-side collection), GTM, Meta, and other marketing pixels.

QA packs

Red / yellow / green results for page-load essentials, Adobe/GA4 parity, a checkout smoke test, a PII guardrail, and Consent Mode.

Golden baselines

Pin a known-good session and compare the next pass with a before/after variable diff.

Proof Timeline

A screenshot of every page and click, with the pixels that fired at each step — a record of the journey you actually walked.

Evidence export

A print/PDF report plus CSV and JSON, and an analytics export of every GA4 and Adobe parameter by page name.

PII checks

Flags only data that was actually sent in tracking requests, including encoded or hashed values. Human review is still required.

Duplicate-hit detector

Surfaces matching hits that fire too close together, so repeated listeners or route events are easier to catch.

Vendor filter

Focus the session with per-vendor chips and search, then export what you were looking at.
